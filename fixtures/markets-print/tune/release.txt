HALCYON MILLS CORP. REPORTS FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2027 RESULTS
Minneapolis, Minn., October 7, 2026

Net sales of $2.41 billion rose 3 percent from the prior-year quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.42, compared with $1.31 a year ago.
Adjusted diluted earnings per share were $1.47, excluding restructuring charges of $0.05 per share.
Gross margin was 31.8 percent, up 220 basis points from the prior year.
The company repurchased 2.1 million shares for $118 million during the quarter.

Outlook
The company now expects full-year diluted earnings per share of $5.60 to $5.80, up from its prior range of $5.40 to $5.60.
Full-year net sales growth is expected to be 2 to 3 percent.

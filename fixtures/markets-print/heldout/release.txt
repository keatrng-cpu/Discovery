TERRAFIELD FOODS INC.
Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Omaha, Neb. | August 12, 2026

Highlights
- Revenue was $1,184 million, an increase of 5.2% over the second quarter of 2025.
- Earnings per diluted share were $2.03, versus $1.88 in the prior-year period.
- Operating cash flow was $212 million.
- The quarter included a one-time gain of $0.07 per share from the sale of the Dayton plant.
- The Board approved a quarterly dividend of $0.54 per share.

Guidance
Terrafield now forecasts full-year earnings per diluted share between $7.95 and $8.15, compared with its prior range of $7.70 to $7.90.
The company expects fiscal 2027 capital expenditures of approximately $310 million.

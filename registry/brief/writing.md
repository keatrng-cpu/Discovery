# Shelf: writing (writing and editorial; verbatim from the owner's spec)

## Short form
Writing. Outline: claims and order, no prose. Draft: voice file, one claim per paragraph, no new claims. Cut: claim count flat or down. Variant: channel only, facts frozen. Series: brand file is memory. Fact: source or unsupported.

## Directive definitions
Outline. List claims. List the order. No prose. A claim without a slot is cut.
Draft. Follow the voice file. Avoid the banned defaults. One claim from the outline per paragraph. No new claims.
Cut. Shorten without adding a claim. Claim count stays flat or falls. Do not restyle into a new piece.
Variant. Change channel, not facts. Keep the offer. Keep the numbers. Voice file still applies.
Series. Load the brand file. Do not restate the rules in the draft. Match the previous piece's bans. Session chat is not the memory.
Fact. Attach a source or label unsupported. Do not upgrade a label by tone. Cut or label. No third option.

## Status and ceiling
* Outline. Reliable. Claims and order, no prose, is already a good constraint. Stays reliable.
* Draft. Assisted. Voice files help. New claims appear. Banned defaults slip without a checker. Medium chance.
* Cut. Assisted to reliable. Claim count can be a diff. High chance.
* Variant. Assisted. Channel change with frozen facts works if facts are a list. Medium-high chance.
* Series. Assisted. A brand file beats session memory. High chance as a harness rule.
* Fact. Assisted. Source-or-label is enforceable and still leaked. Same citation problem as research. Medium chance.

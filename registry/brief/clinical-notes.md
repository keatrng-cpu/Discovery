# Shelf: clinical-notes (clinical notes; verbatim from the owner's spec)

## Short form
Clinical notes. Documentation only. Transcribe template, mark missing. Gap names the empty field. Cite cuts a line the transcript does not hold. Sign presents and stops. No diagnosis, no plan of care.

## Directive definitions
Documentation only. Not a diagnosis, not a plan of care.
Transcribe. Fill template fields from the transcript. Mark missing fields missing. Do not smooth gaps into findings.
Gap. Name the empty field. Ask for that field. Do not invent it.
Cite. Each line traces to the transcript. A line that does not is cut. No imported history unless spoken.
Sign. Present the note. Stop. The clinician signs.

## Status and ceiling
Documentation only. Ambient-scribe products already draft notes. They do not own the finding.
* Transcribe. Assisted to reliable into a template. Smoothing gaps into findings is the failure. Medium-high chance the template holds.
* Gap. Assisted. Naming an empty field works. Inventing it is the failure. Medium chance.
* Cite. Assisted. Line-to-transcript is checkable and often skipped. Medium chance.
* Sign. Stop. Low chance a clinician stops signing. This directive must not become a diagnosis.

# Shelf: audio (audio and speech; verbatim from the owner's spec)

## Short form
Audio. Cut one region. Names from a list, flag missing, do not guess. Score: temp music under rules, hook only. Assemble approved takes, no regenerate, human listen. Ceiling closed.

## Directive definitions
Cut. Tighten one region. Keep the take. Do not restitch the episode. Listen to that region.
Names. Read the pronunciation list. Flag a name not on the list. Do not guess a proper noun. List wins.
Score. Place temp music under the rule file. Check the hook only. Do not compose a new theme in this directive.
Assemble. Order approved takes. Do not regenerate audio. Human listen is the close. Ceiling stays shut.

## Status and ceiling
* Cut. Assisted. One region is editable. Episode restitch is the failure mode to forbid. Medium chance.
* Names. Reliable if the list is a tool. Guessing is the failure. High chance.
* Score. Assisted temp placement. New-theme generation is out of this directive on purpose. Medium chance.
* Assemble. Assisted order of approved takes. Human listen stays the close. Low chance that close disappears.

# Shelf: sales (sales and marketing; verbatim from the owner's spec)

## Short form
Sales. Frame: buyer, job, what they pay for, drop if no buyer. Cluster: quote under each group. Question: falsifiable, do not answer in the same directive. Brief: public resolved facts, likely objection, no invented metrics. Asset: winning frame only. Test: smallest spend and kill result. Send and spend denied.

## Directive definitions
Frame. Name the buyer. Name the job. Name what they pay for. A frame without a buyer is dropped.
Cluster. Group quotes. Keep a quote under each group. Do not invent a segment the quotes do not support.
Question. Ask what would explain the gap. Make it falsifiable. Do not answer it in the same directive.
Brief. Use the prospect's public facts. Name the likely objection. No invented metrics. Prepared before the meeting, not after.
Asset. Write for the winning frame only. Name the segment in the brief. Name the objection it handles. Do not produce the other frames.
Test. Name the smallest spend or send. Name what result would kill the frame. Gate before either happens.

## Status and ceiling
No clean benchmark. The useful mechanism is clustering and question generation, not asset volume.
* Frame. Assisted. Buyer, job, and payment are writable. Frames without a buyer still appear. Medium chance.
* Cluster. Assisted to reliable if a quote must sit under each group. High chance with that rule.
* Question. Reliable as a question. It answers in the same breath unless blocked. High chance the block holds.
* Brief. Assisted. Public facts work. Invented metrics are the failure. Medium chance.
* Asset. Assisted once a frame has won. Producing all frames is the waste. Medium chance.
* Test. Assisted plan, gated send and spend. Low chance the gate opens.

# Shelf: education (verbatim from the owner's spec)

## Short form
Education. Explain: one concept, one example, then a question. Check: restate or apply, on a miss change the example. Sequence: smallest gap only. Drill: one skill, mark answers, do not advance. Curriculum: propose order, person sets it.

## Directive definitions
Explain. One concept. One example. No second concept. A question follows.
Check. Ask for a restate or one application. Mark it. On a miss, change the example. Do not repeat the same paragraph.
Sequence. Name the smallest gap. Name the next step only. Do not lay out the whole course.
Drill. One skill. Mark each answer. Do not move on inside the same directive.
Curriculum. Propose an order. A person sets it. Autonomous curricula stay closed.

## Status and ceiling
* Explain. Assisted to reliable for one concept plus one example. Second concepts leak. Medium-high chance.
* Check. Assisted. Restate-and-mark works. It repeats the paragraph on a miss unless the example is required to change. Medium chance.
* Sequence. Assisted. Smallest-gap works. Whole-course dumps are the failure. Medium chance.
* Drill. Assisted. Marking works if answers are known. Medium chance.
* Curriculum. Gated. Low chance a person stops ordering it.

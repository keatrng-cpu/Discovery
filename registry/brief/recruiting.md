# Shelf: recruiting (verbatim from the owner's spec)

## Short form
Recruiting. Scorecard from the req, manager accepts, no extra traits. Extract those fields only, blanks blank, quote the resume line. Screen note per line, evidence or blank. Compare on the same lines, missing stays missing. Decide prepares the sheet and stops. No hire tool.

## Directive definitions
Scorecard. Take lines from the req. A manager accepts the lines. No extra traits. The req wins.
Extract. Fill only scorecard fields. Leave blanks blank. No inferred personality. Quote the resume line used.
Screen note. One note per scorecard line. Evidence or blank. No overall vibe score.
Compare. Same lines for each candidate. Missing evidence stays missing. Do not rank on fluency.
Decide. Prepare the sheet. Stop. A human hires.

## Status and ceiling
* Scorecard. Assisted. Req lines work. Extra traits appear unless forbidden. Medium-high chance.
* Extract. Assisted. Blanks stay blank only under a schema. Inferred traits are the failure. Medium chance.
* Screen note. Assisted. One note per line works. Vibe scores return if an overall field exists. Medium chance.
* Compare. Assisted. Missing-as-missing works. Fluency bias remains. Medium chance the sheet is fairer. Low chance the bias is gone.
* Decide. Stop. Low chance a model hires by 2027 in any defensible process.

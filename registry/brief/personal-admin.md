# Shelf: personal-admin (personal admin; verbatim from the owner's spec)

## Short form
Personal admin. Triage headers by rule. Draft one thread, stop before send. Brief: real overlaps only. Pack: source per item. Book and pay prepares details. Send, book, and pay denied.

## Directive definitions
Triage. Read headers first. Open only what the rule marks. Record why it was opened. Do not reply yet.
Draft. Reply to one thread. Match the ask. Stop before send.
Brief. List real overlaps. Name the two events. Do not call a back-to-back a conflict unless times overlap.
Pack. One agenda item per source. Source attached. No item from memory alone.
Book and pay. Prepare the details. Stop. Always gated.

## Status and ceiling
* Triage. Assisted to reliable on headers plus a rule. High chance.
* Draft. Assisted. Match-the-ask works. Send stays gated. High chance the draft is routine. Low chance unattended send is acceptable.
* Brief. Assisted. Real overlaps need calendar data, not prose. Medium-high chance.
* Pack. Assisted. Source-attached items work. Memory-only items are the failure. Medium chance.
* Book and pay. Stop. Low chance the gate opens.
